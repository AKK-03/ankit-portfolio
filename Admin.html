Page switches

Page live shows or hides a page for visitors (they see your message instead). Sender starts or stops the scheduled reminder emails of that page. The two switches are independent, so you can hide a page but keep sending, or the other way round.

Add another page

Register any other page (for example portfolio) so you can switch it on or off. Add one line to that page to obey the switch: fetch(API+'?action=page_status&page=portfolio').

Sender details

These appear on every Jobs and Family reminder email, and replies go to the Reply-To address. WhatsApp messages get your name and phone at the end.

Sender switches

Turn the scheduled reminder emails of each page on or off. Switched-off senders send nothing, and Health & logs will not report them as failures.

Note: the “From” address is always the Google account that owns the Apps Script project. Google does not allow changing it, but the name and Reply-To you set here are what people see and reply to. To send from another address, deploy the script under that Google account.

🔒 Privacy: what recipients can see

Untick anything you do not want shown. It is removed from the footer of every message. This does not change who replies go to (Reply-To above).

💬 WhatsApp / mobile delivery

Messages go through CallMeBot. Every phone number needs its own API key: the person must first message the CallMeBot contact (instructions at callmebot.com, “Free WhatsApp API”) and CallMeBot replies with a key. Add each number and its key here. Before this fix one shared key was used for everybody, so it only worked for one number.
